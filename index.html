Type Page

A typewriter for your browser. A4 sheets, English and Bangla.

Page 1 of 1
Click the page, then press any key to begin

Every sheet is A4. When a page is full, typing moves on to a new sheet, and Next sheet jumps to the next one. Click anywhere on a page to put the cursor there. Arrow keys, Home, End, Delete, Page Up and Page Down move or delete as in Word. Insert pushes text right; Overstrike types over it like a real typewriter. Enter splits the line at the cursor and pushes the rest (and the lines below) down, and Backspace at the start of a line joins it back, as in Word. In Overstrike mode Enter just moves to the next line. Backspace erases is on: it deletes the letter before the cursor.
Bangla: choose বাংলা, then type phonetically. Examples: ami = আমি, bangla = বাংলা, kh = খ, Ng = ঙ, ng = ং, T = ট, o stops letters joining (kot = কত, kto = ক্ত), . = ।
